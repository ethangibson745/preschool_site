Our Objectives

  • To provide a good quality education for the children.
  • To provide affordable sessional childcare.
  • To provide a stimulating and encouraging environment.
  • To include parents/carers whenever possible in all aspects of playschool life.
  • To have trained and enthusiastic staff.
  • To offer children the chance to mix and play with other children.
  • To support each child to fulfil their individual potential.
  • To follow the overarching aims of the Early Years Foundation Stage to help children achieve the five Every Child Matters Outcomes.
  • Our curriculum is based on Development Matters.
